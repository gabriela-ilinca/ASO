# Classical Add-On Product Analysis

## Executive Summary

This analysis evaluates a new product differentiation strategy: offering discounted Classical concert tickets to single ticket buyers who attend Special or Holiday events. The strategy preserves existing pricing while creating a new "add-on" product that can be tested without cannibalizing current demand.

**Key Findings:**
- **Target Market Size:** 7,008 Special/Holiday single ticket buyers (30% of all single ticket accounts)
- **Untapped Potential:** 80.6% of these buyers have never purchased a Classical ticket
- **Projected Incremental Revenue:** $102,099 from 1,926 new Classical tickets
- **CYO Conversion Pipeline:** 50 projected new CYO subscribers from the program

---

## 1. Product Design

### Discount Structure (Cumulative)

| Purchase Level | Discount | Price Example (Avg $58.56) |
|----------------|----------|---------------------------|
| +1 Classical ticket | 5% off | $55.63 |
| +2 Classical tickets | 10% off each | $52.70 per ticket |
| +3 Classical tickets | 15% off each | $49.78 per ticket |

### Offer Validity
- **60-day window** from the date of attending a Special/Holiday event
- Creates urgency while allowing time for upcoming Classical concerts

### Eligible Events
- All regular series Classical concerts

## 2. Marketing Strategy: Why Same-Day Offers Matter

### The Psychology of Timing

Offering this discount **on the same day** the customer attends a Special/Holiday event is critical for several reasons:

1. **Peak Emotional Engagement:** Customers are at their highest point of satisfaction immediately after a positive concert experience

2. **Brand Association:** The memory of the Special/Holiday event is fresh, making the Classical offer feel like a natural continuation

3. **Reduced Decision Friction:** Post-concert customers have already demonstrated willingness to spend time and money on ASO; adding one more purchase is an easier decision than a cold outreach weeks later

4. **Social Proof:** If attending with companions, group decisions to "try another concert" are more likely in the moment

### Recommended Touchpoints

| Channel | Timing | Message |
|---------|--------|---------|
| Email | Within 2 hours post-event | "Continue your ASO experience..." |
| In-venue signage | During intermission/exit | QR code to add-on offer |
| Box office | At event check-in | Staff mention of add-on program |
| SMS (if opted in) | Next morning | Reminder with upcoming Classical options |

## 6. Risk Mitigation

### Why Product Differentiation Protects Existing Demand

The critical design principle of this program is that discounts are offered **only for the new add-on product**, not existing single ticket pricing:

1. **No cannibalization:** Customers who would have bought Classical tickets at full price still do so
2. **Isolated testing:** If the program underperforms, only the add-on product is affected
3. **Price integrity:** Full-price Classical tickets remain available; the discount is positioned as a "loyalty reward" for Special/Holiday attendees
4. **Clear segmentation:** The offer is only available to verified Special/Holiday ticket holders, preventing general discount expectations

### Monitoring Recommendations

Track these metrics to evaluate program success:

| Metric | Target | Red Flag |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Response rate | >15% | <10% |
| Redemption rate (offers claimed) | >5% of responses | <2% |
| Full-price Classical cannibalization | <2% decline | >5% decline |
| CYO conversion rate (from 3+ buyers) | >20% | <15% |
