# General Presentation Tips

1. **Pacing:** Allow pauses for audience to absorb data-heavy slides
2. **Eye contact:** Look up from notes regularly
3. **Transitions:** Use phrases like "Building on this..." and "This leads us to..." between sections
4. **Questions:** Pause for questions at section breaks (after Audience Behavior, Revenue, Subscriber Movement)
5. **Emphasis:** When showing dollar figures, slow down and let them land
6. **Confidence:** You know this data - speak with authority

# Key Messages to Reinforce Throughout

1. **CYO is disproportionately valuable** - small group, big revenue
2. **Single ticket buyers are growing** - opportunity to convert them
3. **Product differentiation protects pricing** - discounts are targeted, not broad
4. **Data-driven recommendations** - everything is backed by analysis
5. **Conservative projections** - actual results may exceed estimates
