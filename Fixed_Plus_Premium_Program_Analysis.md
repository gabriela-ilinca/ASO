# Fixed Plus Premium Program Analysis

## Executive Summary

This analysis evaluates a premium "Fixed Plus" program targeting Fixed 6 subscribers - ASO's core subscription base. The program offers advance access to Holiday and Special events at a 10% premium, bundled with exclusive benefits.

**Key Findings:**
- **Fixed 6 subscribers are underserved:** 57.8% already purchase additional single tickets (proving demand exists)
- **Strong Holiday/Special appetite:** 381 Fixed 6 accounts bought Special single tickets, 196 bought Holiday
- **Timing insight:** 45% buy 2+ months in advance - "advance access" is genuinely valuable to them
- **Premium seat loyalty:** 83% of Fixed 6 subscriptions are in premium sections - they value quality
- **Projected Revenue:** $44,310 from 354 Fixed Plus subscribers, with $4,028 pure premium captured

---

## 1. Fixed 6 Subscriber Behavior Analysis

### Why Target Fixed 6?

Fixed 6 is the largest Fixed subscription tier with **1,643 accounts** (70% of all Fixed subscribers). They represent the "sweet spot":
- Committed enough to subscribe (not casual single-ticket buyers)
- But not maxed out (unlike Fixed 24 subscribers who attend everything)
- **Room to grow** - they want more, but current offerings don't serve them

| Fixed Tier | Total Accounts | % Buying Single Tickets | Avg Events | Avg Revenue |
|------------|---------------|------------------------|------------|-------------|
| Fixed 6 | 1,643 | **57.8%** | 2.5 | $355 |
| Fixed 12 | 199 | 70.9% | 2.6 | $427 |
| Fixed 18 | 62 | 69.4% | 2.9 | $428 |
| Fixed 24 | 137 | 70.8% | 2.6 | $346 |

**Key Insight:** 57.8% of Fixed 6 subscribers (950 accounts) purchase additional single tickets - this is **proven demand** for content beyond their subscription.

### What Events Are They Buying?

Among Fixed 6 subscribers who buy single tickets:

| Event Type | Unique Buyers | Total Seats | Total Revenue |
|------------|--------------|-------------|---------------|
| Classical | 709 | 3,274 | $208,590 |
| **Special** | **381** | **951** | **$82,806** |

**Key Insight:** 381 Fixed 6 accounts bought Special event single tickets, and 196 bought Holiday. Combined, that's **over 500 accounts** demonstrating appetite for these premium events.

## 2. What Do Fixed 6 Subscribers Want?

### Evidence from Behavior

1. **They want MORE events** - 57.8% buy additional single tickets averaging 2.5 extra events

2. **They plan ahead** - Purchase timing analysis shows:
   - 45.4% buy single tickets 2+ months in advance
   - Median: 40 days before event
   - **"Advance access" is genuinely valuable to them**

3. **They value premium seating**:
   - 82.8% of subscription seats are in premium sections (Orchestra/Loge)
   - 80.3% of their single ticket purchases are also premium
   - **They consistently choose quality over economy**

4. **They're willing to pay more** - Average single ticket spend: $355 per buyer

### Pain Points to Solve

Based on the behavioral data, Fixed 6 subscribers likely experience:

| Pain Point | Evidence | Solution |
|------------|----------|----------|
| **FOMO on Special Events** | 381 bought Special single tickets | Guaranteed access |
| **Holiday sellouts** | 196 bought Holiday single tickets | Priority booking |
| **Seat uncertainty** | 80%+ buy premium seats | Guaranteed premium seating |
| **Hassle of buying extras** | 57.8% already do it anyway | Bundle convenience |
| **No recognition** | Same treatment as everyone else | VIP status benefits |

## 6. Why This Works (Behavioral Economics)

### 1. Bundling Effect
Customers perceive bundles as better value even at the same total price. "Fixed Plus" feels like an upgrade, not an add-on purchase.

### 2. Loss Aversion
"Priority Access" and "Sellout Protection" frame the benefit as avoiding a loss (missing out) rather than gaining something - which is psychologically more motivating.

### 3. Status Signaling
"Fixed Plus Member" recognition appeals to identity and belonging. Subscribers aren't just buying tickets; they're joining an exclusive group.

### 4. Commitment & Consistency
Once someone identifies as a "Fixed Plus" member, they're more likely to renew and upgrade further (consistency with self-image).

### 5. Convenience Premium
People willingly pay more for convenience. Single checkout + flex swap removes friction that might otherwise cause them to skip add-on purchases.

## 8. Risk Mitigation

### Cannibalization Risk

**Concern:** Will Fixed Plus just shift existing single ticket revenue at a discount?

**Mitigation:**
- 10% premium means no revenue loss on conversions
- Primary targets are people who would have bought anyway (capture premium)
- Secondary targets are non-ST buyers (net new revenue)

### Operational Complexity

**Concern:** Managing separate inventory for Fixed Plus priority access

**Mitigation:**
- Start with modest holds (5% of Holiday/Special capacity)
- Expand based on demand
- Technology: Simple flag in ticketing system

### Benefit Delivery

**Concern:** Can we actually deliver on pre-concert talks, lounge access, etc.?

**Mitigation:**
- Phase benefits over time
- Start with low-cost/no-cost benefits (priority access, recognition)
- Add experiential benefits as program matures

## 10. Conclusion

Fixed 6 subscribers represent ASO's most loyal yet underserved segment. The data proves they want more:

- **57.8% already buy single tickets** - demand exists
- **45% plan 2+ months ahead** - advance access is valuable
- **83% choose premium seats** - quality matters

Fixed Plus meets them where they are by:
1. Bundling what they already want (Holiday + Special events)
2. Adding genuine value (priority access, guaranteed seating)
3. Creating VIP status (recognition, exclusive benefits)
4. Charging a modest premium (10%) that feels justified

**Projected outcome:** 354 Fixed Plus subscribers generating $44,310 in premium-priced revenue, with a pathway to expand benefits and pricing in future seasons.
